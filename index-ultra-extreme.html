01 · About

Where field reality
meets data intelligence.

A short story about how I ended up writing code in Bihar's most data-rich, least-digitised spaces — and why that matters.

I'm a public health data professional and full-stack developer currently working as a Gandhi Fellow with Piramal Foundation in Muzaffarpur, Bihar. My day starts in the field — coordinating with ASHA workers, ANMs, PHC/CHC staff, and district officials — and ends with Python scripts, SQL queries, and data pipelines that turn raw field data into decisions.

I work across the entire data lifecycle: from ODK Collect & ODK Central forms in the field, through cleaning and validation in Python, to MIS reports that land on the desks of district officials. Outside of health data, I've built and deployed production MERN stack apps with JWT auth, REST APIs, Nginx, and PM2 — and I use AI tools daily to sharpen both workflows.

My edge is simple: I've actually been in the field, so I know what the data means before it ever hits a spreadsheet.
